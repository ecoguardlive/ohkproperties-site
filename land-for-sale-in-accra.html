Plots of land at Kwabenya and around Accra

Kwabenya is in the north of Accra, close to Haatso, Dome and Atomic Junction, and is popular with buyers building a family home or holding land as an investment. We list land at Kwabenya, Kwabenya Comet and Kwabenya ACP, and add new plots as they become available.

What to check before you buy land in Accra

  • Ask to see the title documents and the site plan, and confirm the exact size and boundaries on the ground.
  • Have the land searched at the Lands Commission before you make any payment.
  • Confirm access: is there a motorable road, and are water and electricity close by?
  • Visit the plot in person, ideally with a lawyer or a licensed surveyor.

A standard plot in and around Accra is commonly about 70 ft by 100 ft (roughly 0.16 acre), but sizes vary, so always confirm the dimensions of the specific plot.
