Common questions

Buying land and renting in Accra: your questions answered

How do I buy land in Accra safely?

Ask to see the title documents and the site plan, check the exact boundaries on the ground, and have the land searched at the Lands Commission before you pay. Taking a lawyer or a licensed surveyor with you is a sensible extra step. Contact us and we will share what is available for each plot.

How big is a plot of land in Accra?

In and around Accra a standard plot is commonly about 70 ft by 100 ft, roughly 0.16 acre, but sizes vary by area and by seller. Always confirm the exact dimensions on the site plan. Our listings show whether a plot is a half plot, a full plot or several plots.

Do you have land for sale in Kwabenya?

Yes. We currently list land at Kwabenya, Kwabenya Comet and Kwabenya ACP. Browse the land for sale in Accra page or message us for the latest plots.

Can I rent a house or apartment through Ohk Properties?

Yes. See our homes to rent in Accra, then message us to confirm availability, pricing and what is included before arranging a viewing.
